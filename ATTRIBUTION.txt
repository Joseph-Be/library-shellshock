Lab container sources
=====================
The files under provisioning/roles/shellshock-lab/files/Labsetup/ are the
original SEED lab container sources (docker-compose.yml, image_www/Dockerfile,
vul.cgi, getenv.cgi, server_name.conf and the intentionally vulnerable
bash_shellshock binary provided by SEED), used unchanged except for:
  - a small flag-entrypoint.sh that writes the per-sandbox flags inside the
    container and then starts Apache;
  - environment / healthcheck additions in docker-compose.yml.

The base image handsonsecurity/seed-server:apache-php is published by the SEED
project on Docker Hub.
